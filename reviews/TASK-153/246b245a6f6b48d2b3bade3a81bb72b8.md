## Blocking review finding: invalid positive workflow assumptions

The owner-managed Devlegate workflow is `backlog → todo → review → accepted
→ done`; `done` follows successful integration from `accepted`.
Deferral/discard belongs in the supported `backlog` or `abandoned` paths
under their applicable owner protocols. A raw `todo → done`, `todo →
accepted`, or `todo → review` Git move is not an ordinary completed
Devlegate lifecycle transition. An externally edited repository can contain
such states, but a **positive test** must not canonize the invalid move as
successful orchestrator behavior.

Precisely which tests/branches should be removed or reduced:

1. **`tests/test_control_recovery.py ::
   test_worker_ownership_scopes_published_failure_visibility`**
   (new in this execution, approximately 85 lines).
   **Keep the regression itself**, namely an old, provably superseded failure
   T-OLD remaining absent from human/JSON status while an unrelated T-NEW
   worker is active, after restart, without erasing historical evidence and
   without authorizing retry. **Replace** its direct
   `kanban/todo/T-1.md → kanban/done/T-1.md` with a supported published
   transition (e.g. deferral to `backlog`; a `done` fixture must follow a
   legal `review → accepted → done` lifecycle). **Remove from this long
   test** the extra unrelated product dirty/commit/push experiment
   (`# Product observations belong to admission`), the two-case
   `execution_ref_retirement` branch-owner loop, and redundant repeated
   ownership/marker permutations. Retain one concise matching-owner or
   unresolved-intent fail-closed assertion, using existing coverage for
   other admission/product/ownership cases. Do not delete this whole test.

2. **`tests/test_control_recovery.py ::
   test_published_ticket_transition_supersedes_only_current_failure`**
   (pre-existing test, expanded here).
   **Remove** positive parameter cases `destination="review"`,
   `"accepted"`, and `"done"` as they all move straight from
   `todo` without the actual lifecycle. **Remove** `"deleted"` as an
   ordinary successful retirement path; raw deletion is not a supported
   alternative to `backlog`/`abandoned`. If deletion is relevant as a
   hostile external edit, cover it in a small *negative/fail-closed* test,
   rather than positive lifecycle coverage. Keep `"backlog"` and the
   `"todo"` deferral/requeue scenario, and consider a supported
   `abandoned` case if the existing lifecycle fixture can produce it
   correctly. Do not add a universal path-transition validator here.
   **Remove the three unrelated `--allow-empty` commits and exact
   `ls-tree == 3` subprocess-count assertion**: these are fragile
   implementation-specific performance checks, not the functional
   regression. Remove the `destination == "done"`-only dirty,
   remote-ref rewrite and unreadable-history blocks along with that case,
   keeping fail-closed observation coverage in a focused valid-state test.

3. **`tests/test_control_recovery.py ::
   test_changed_control_without_immutable_transition_stays_blocked`**.
   The newly added `change="edit-before-move"` sequence is an additional
   malformed, externally manipulated history; it is **not necessary** to
   prove the user-visible incident once `"unrelated"`, `"edited"` and
   one explicit `"recreated"` ambiguous-identity negative remain. Drop
   `"edit-before-move"` unless it uniquely reveals an actual regression
   in the shared observation; do not expand this matrix further.
   Retain the deletion/recreation identity check (negative) because it is
   meaningfully different from an ordinary content edit.

4. **Tests already present in the integrated base are not mandatory
   deletion targets for this narrow rework.** In particular
   `test_side_branch_deletion_and_divergent_remote_do_not_supersede_failure`
   and `test_failed_generation_product_lineage_and_ticket_transition`
   predate the current production fix. Do not rewrite/delete the entire
   existing product-lineage safety suite just to lower the diff count.
   Equally, retain the small same-generation live-owner assertion in
   `tests/test_service_snapshot.py`; it tests an important distinct
   safety invariant. The goal is removal of **new or actively expanded
   redundant coverage**, not erasure of safety tests.
