## Mandatory dependent checks

- Warning/recovery thresholds; stdout/stderr partial-byte resets; output arriving at timeout; normal completion; live owned descendant suppression; unknown cgroup membership fail-closed; accurate operator stalled/recovering state.
- Durable old-attempt stalled reason, signal only after durable intent, exact cgroup retirement proof, no overlapping/duplicate replacements, preserved dirty workspace and historical reports; service restart at intent, signal, retirement and continuation phases; existing stop/reset/retry/process-loss semantics.
- Deterministic recovery-context bytes/path limits, stable formatting and truncation, no private identity leaks; full CI and Ruff once the lifecycle path exists.
- **Zombie edge case under the existing active-descendant acceptance criterion:** an exited/unreaped zombie is *not* an active tool and must not postpone an otherwise eligible stall or a kernel-proven empty-cgroup retirement. Linux cgroup v2 omits zombies from `cgroup.procs`; `cgroup.events: populated=0` means no **live/non-zombie** members. A worker process that has itself exited/zombified belongs to the ordinary exit/retirement path, not the alive-but-idle classification. Test the behavior with a targeted Linux/fixture regression where practical; do **not** add host-wide `ps`/`/proc` scanning, zombie-count thresholds, forced reaping of unrelated processes, or a requirement to wait for all zombies to disappear. Kernel reference: https://github.com/torvalds/linux/blob/master/Documentation/admin-guide/cgroup-v2.rst (Processes; [Un]populated Notification).

## Optional / non-blocking: simplify redundant double cgroup reading

The existing `ExecutionContainment.has_other_processes()` recursively reads the whole cgroup hierarchy **twice**, requires identical snapshots, then returns whether a PID other than the worker was seen. This is unnecessarily expensive and not a meaningful atomicity guarantee: child processes can fork/exit immediately after the second read, and process/hierarchy changes can happen between individual reads. Two agreeing scans are **not** an ownership lease or stable proof. They must not be treated as authorizing a signal or replacement.

Prefer one bounded, fail-closed membership observation, combined with a **fresh** observation and revalidation at the owner decision boundary, followed by exact containment retirement and a kernel-confirmed `cgroup.events: populated=0` before replacement. If the two-scan helper is simplified, do it while connecting that caller, **not as another standalone cleanup execution**. Preserve rejection of unreadable/ambiguous identity, foreign membership and unsafe paths. This observation is secondary to the missing durable stalled recovery.
