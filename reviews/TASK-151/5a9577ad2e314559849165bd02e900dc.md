   256 KiB page. Reconstructing a 1 GiB object in 4096 pages can reread roughly
   4 TiB of source bytes. Preserve the exact source/digest and strict page
   bounds, but use efficient sequential or session-bounded verification
   without O(file-size × page-count) rescanning; enforce explicit owner-side
