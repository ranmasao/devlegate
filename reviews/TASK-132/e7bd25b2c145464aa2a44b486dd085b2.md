## Blocking defect

The operator's high-level reset is intended specifically to stop a currently
working agent, discard its prior attempt/code/context and restart from the
current product/model. In this **ordinary** case its worktree typically has
edited tracked source files and possibly newly created untracked files.

However `src/devlegate/clean_reset.py:retire_ticket_generation()` requires
`binding.require_local(clean=True, read_only=True)` and subsequently
`_require_no_hidden_changes(manager, manager.path)` before retirement.
`ResourceBinding._observe_local(clean=True)` rejects
`workspace.dirty` (raising `ExecutionWorkspaceError`).
Normal in-progress worker edits therefore cause the reset to stop its worker
but block old-resource retirement and prevent the fresh execution. This is
confirmed by the explicit negative
`test_reset_dirty_generation_is_blocked_and_resumable` and dirty
`test_reset_recursive_submodule_safety`; the positive active-worker test
writes its marker **outside** the managed worktree.

The existing blanket policy in `docs/reset.md` that dirty/untracked/ignored
content always blocks reset contradicts its primary user goal. It is not
acceptable to make an operator manually clean the worktree before the
one-argument reset. **A proved old generation being deliberately abandoned
is distinct from a replaced, foreign or unproven namespace.**
