# Contributing to Devlegate

Thank you for contributing. Keep changes focused, preserve deterministic
behavior, and include focused tests for behavioral changes.

## Developer Certificate of Origin

Devlegate uses the Developer Certificate of Origin, version 1.1, instead of a
Contributor License Agreement. By contributing, you certify that you have the
right to submit the work under the applicable project scope. The canonical DCO
text is in [DCO](DCO).

Sign each commit with your real name and email using:

```text
git commit -s
```

The commit must contain a trailer in this form:

```text
Signed-off-by: Name <email>
```

This project follows an inbound-equals-outbound policy without copyright
assignment. Contributions to EUPL-scoped Devlegate implementation are submitted
under EUPL-1.2. Contributions to CC0-scoped copyable and default material are
submitted under CC0-1.0. Contributors certify that they have the right to
submit their contribution under the applicable scope.

NanoYAML remains a separately licensed upstream project under MIT. Do not
silently turn NanoYAML material into Devlegate-owned material or change its

There is no CLA and no copyright assignment. Contributors retain ownership of
their contributions, subject to the applicable outbound license.
